Think of it as a chain of tiny decision-makers. Each one looks at the input, makes a small judgment, and passes it along. After training on millions of examples, the chain gets good at recognizing patterns — faces, words, even tone.
Field notes

Everything happens on your machine.

No data sent out, no monthly fee to keep using it. A fast, private assistant that's on even when your connection isn't.

Completely private

Your conversations never leave your machine. No telemetry, no training on your data, no corporate servers involved.

Genuinely fast

Responses in seconds. Running locally means no network lag, no queue, and no rate limits to wait out.

Optional web search

When you need current info, Pallas can search the web and pull in live results — no API key required.

Chat history

Create an account to save and revisit past conversations, stored locally on your own device.

Reads your hardware

Pallas checks your GPU and RAM automatically and pulls a model sized to fit — no configuration needed.

Multiple models

Switch between Pallas 1 (fast, 3B), Pallas 1.1 (balanced, 7B) and Pallas 1.2 Max any time.

Specifications

Three instruments, one machine.

Every model runs on your own hardware. No GPU required — though it helps with the larger ones.
